# Bid1

Dallas Independent School District is procuring Student and Staff Computing Devices under Bid JA-207652, SOURCING #168884, 00004079100, including Chromebook laptops, Windows laptops, tablets, staff laptops, staff desktops, and touch and non-touch monitors. The bid is due July 9, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST, after Addendum 2 changed the original due date of June 27, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST. A pre-bid meeting was held on June 10, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST via TEAMS video conference, and attendance was highly encouraged but not stated to be mandatory. Submission is preferred electronically through the iSupplier portal, but manual submission is allowed as one original hard copy in a sealed envelope; fax and email submissions are not accepted. Deployment services are required, including white glove services, asset decaling, asset reporting, etching, delivery to varied locations, software installation, asset tagging and etching, device deployment and setup, and asset control reporting; Windows devices require autopilot, Chromebooks require enrollment in Google Management, and etching is required only on laptops. Delivery from awarded vendors is to begin in September 2024, the term is a three-year agreement with two successive one-year extensions not to exceed five years total, and the bid references EPCNT and CTPA as cooperative/interlocal purchasing opportunities. Documentation, manufacturer authorization, and warranty requirements are not stated in the provided documents.
