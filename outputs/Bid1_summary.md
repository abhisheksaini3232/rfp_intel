# Bid1

Dallas Independent School District is procuring student and staff computing devices under Bid JA-207652; SOURCING #168884; 00004079100, titled “Student and Staff Computing Devices,” with target quantities covering student Chromebooks, student Windows laptops, student tablets, staff laptops, staff desktops, and display monitors. The due date is July 9, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST, and the pre-bid meeting was held June 10, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST via TEAMS video conference; attendance was highly encouraged, but mandatory status was not stated. Submissions are preferred electronically through the iSupplier portal, though manual paper submissions in a sealed envelope are also permitted; fax and email submissions are not accepted. Required documentation includes a signed offer/proposal, MWBE forms, W9, Form 1295 unless publicly traded, three business references, signed Addenda 1 and 2, and other conditional items such as reseller certifications, a credit application, and joint-venture materials if applicable. The vendor must provide deployment services including asset decaling, asset reporting, etching, delivery to varied locations, software installation, device deployment and setup, and asset control reporting; etching is required only on laptops, Windows devices require autopilot, and Chromebooks require enrollment in Google Management. All equipment must be new, current model production, and any warranties must be official manufacturer warranties; no manufacturer is named, and no fixed delivery date is stated, though Dallas ISD expects to begin requesting quotes for different projects from awarded vendors starting in September 2024.
