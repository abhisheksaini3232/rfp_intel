# Bid1

Dallas Independent School District is procuring Student and Staff Computing Devices under bid JA-207652 / 168884 / 00004079100, with target quantities spread over the life of the contract for student Chromebooks, student Windows laptops, student tablets, staff laptops, staff desktops, and display monitors. The bid is due July 9, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST after Addendum 2 changed the original June 27, 2024 deadline, and the pre-bid meeting was held June 10, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST via TEAMS, with attendance highly encouraged but not stated as mandatory. Submission is preferred electronically through the iSupplier portal, but manual sealed-paper submissions are also permitted by mail, FedEx, UPS, or delivery to Procurement Services; fax and email submissions are not accepted. Required services include white-glove deployment with asset decaling, asset reporting, etching, and delivery to varied locations, with etching required on laptops only, Windows devices requiring autopilot, and Chromebooks requiring enrollment in Google Management. The term is a three-year agreement with two successive one-year extensions, not to exceed five years total, and the contract may use EPCNT or CTPA; delivery is starting in September 2024 per Addendum 1. Manufacturer authorization, warranty details, and other documentation requirements are not stated in the provided documents.
