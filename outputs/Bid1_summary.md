# Bid1

Dallas Independent School District is procuring student and staff laptops, tablets, desktops, and monitors under RFP 168884 / JA-207652, with a three-year agreement that may be extended for up to two additional years. Addendum 2 moved the proposal deadline to July 9, 2024, at 2:00 PM CST; a TEAMS pre-bid meeting was scheduled for June 10, 2024, at 2:00 PM CST, with attendance highly encouraged but not stated as mandatory. As clarified by Addendum 1, electronic submission through the iSupplier portal is preferred, or vendors may submit one original hard copy in a sealed envelope; fax and email are not accepted. Required documentation includes signed copies of both addenda, MWBE forms regardless of MWBE status, a signed W9, and Form 1295 unless the company is publicly traded; reseller bidders must also provide factory-authorized repair and maintenance certifications, while the insurance form is due within ten days of award. Deployment includes asset tracking, laptop-only etching, software installation and device setup; Addendum 1 specifies Windows Autopilot, Chromebook enrollment in Google Management, and minimum 65 W Chromebook chargers, while device warranty minimums range from one to three years. No fixed delivery date or window is stated: the district anticipated requesting project quotes from awarded vendors starting in September 2024, with delivery deadlines agreed for each project.
